Equality and stability in Steiner’s Vizing bound

A proof-first partial result towards Vizing’s domination conjecture

partial-results problems/vizing-domination 24 July 2026
Abstract We reconstruct the exact optimization behind Steiner’s 0.5643 bound, locate the algebraic failure in the withdrawn 0.5809 claim, and prove a strict instance-sensitive strengthening of Steiner’s key subset lemma. We sharpen terminal equality to singleton and triangle conflict atoms, decompose every unit of product-proof slack, classify formal fibre incidence, and prove orthogonal fractional-tensor and square-clique-cover bounds with exact universal obstructions. We further prove that tight atomic columns have external private targets, that their row holes are two-packings, and that a bidirectional weighted-domination lift certifies every product with P4. Finally, we correct the lift’s deficit algebra, construct connected formal-ratio counterfamilies to all marginal repairs, extract labelled distance-three escape cycles, and prove that a stronger combined lift has zero universal factor on connected Cayley graph pairs. We then prove exact typed partial-cover and fractional repair-energy inequalities, show that the typed relaxation clears the robust Cayley obstruction at every pair of scales, and charge fibre isolation quantitatively to labelled escape obligations, collisions, or cross-coordinate redundancy. Session seven restores typed cardinality slack, proves exact overlap and multiplicity identities, classifies zero-defect escape freedom, and realizes Steiner’s factor-invariant optimizer as an actual graph limit. It also proves a universal adaptive-provider Hall matching whose tight transitions form labelled Eulerian cell cycles and local expansion cuts. No improved universal constant is claimed.

1 · Setup

Let γG(S) be the minimum number of vertices needed to dominate S⊆V(G), and let ρ{2}(G) be the maximum total weight of a nonnegative integer function f satisfying f(N[v])≤2 for every vertex. Define the exact slack

δG(S) = |S| + ρ{2}(G) − 3γG(S).(1)

Steiner’s Lemma 2.3 is precisely δG(S)≥0.

2 · Exact equality and stability

Slack theorem. Suppose q=|N[v]∩S|≥3, put S′=S\N[v] and e=γG(S′)+1− γG(S). Then δ(S)=δ(S′)+(q−3)+3e. At a terminal set meeting every closed neighborhood at most twice, if r=ρG(S), then δ=(ρ{2}−r−γ)+(|S|+r−2γ), with both summands nonnegative integers.
Proof. The first identity is direct subtraction after observing that adding v to a dominator of S′ dominates S. The second is the sum of the slacks in Steiner’s two terminal matching bounds.

Thus equality is recursive: every dense step removes exactly three target vertices and lowers γ by exactly one; the terminal set must make both auxiliary bounds tight. If δ≤2, no dense step can lose a unit of domination, because that would contribute 3 to the slack.

3 · The peeling-defect inequality

Repeatedly choose vi whose closed neighborhood hits the current target in qi≥3 vertices, and delete that hit. Let

pG(S) = max Σi(qi−3). (2)
Peeling-defect inequality. For every finite graph G and S⊆V(G),
3γG(S) ≤ |S| + ρ{2}(G) − pG(S). (3)
Proof. A sequence of length t gives γ(S)≤γ(S t)+t. Apply Steiner’s lemma only to the residual target, substitute |St|=|S|−Σ qi, and maximize the subtracted excess.

4 · Cartesian-product consequence

For the vertically dominated fibre sets L1,…,Lγ(H) in Steiner’s proof, the same counting now yields

γ(G□H) ≥ ¼(3γ(G)−ρ{2}(G))γ(H) + ¼ΣipG (Li).(4)

The last term is new and nonnegative. A universal improvement requires a positive aggregate lower bound for it; none is asserted here.

5 · Two algebraic obstructions

Withdrawn 0.5809 argument. With A=γ(G), B=γ(H), x=A−ρ(G), and y=B−ρ(H), the quoted Chen–Piotrowski–Shreve expression equals A(B−y)+ (A−x)y=AB−xy. The proof replaces it by 3AB−2Ay−2xB+xy. At A=B=10, x=y=7 these are 51 and 69. The 0.5809 claim is unusable.

The natural higher-capacity guess 4γG(S)≤|S|+ ρ{3}(G) also fails on an exact five-vertex witness. The corrected theorem 5γG(S)≤2|S|+ ρ{3}(G) does hold: add Steiner’s two subset bounds and use ρ{3}≥ρ{2}+ρG(S). It yields γ(G□H)≥ (5γ(G)−ρ{3}(G))γ(H)/7. The old exact equality point extends to this new parameter, so the universal constant remains 0.5643 without another structural constraint.

6 · Terminal and fibre equality

For a two-sparse target S, join two target vertices in a conflict graph F when one vertex of G can dominate both. Then

γG(S) = |S|−ν(F),   ρG(S)=α(F). (5)

The inequality α(F)+2ν(F)≥|F| is tight exactly when every component is a complete graph of odd order. Thus every tight terminal remainder first has an odd-clique conflict decomposition.

Capacity-two refinement. Let τ2(F) maximize Σwx over weights in {0,1,2} satisfying wx+ wy≤2 on every edge. Then ρ{2}(G)≥τ2(F)≥ α(F)+|F|−ν(F). Full terminal equality holds only for disjoint unions of K1 and K3.

The second inequality follows from Hall deficiency: weight an appropriate subset of a maximum independent set twice, its neighborhood zero, and all remaining vertices once. Using the terminal defect τ2(F)−2|F|+3ν(F) at the leaves of the peeling recursion gives a strictly stronger parameter p△G(S).

Exact fibre-slack identity. With k=γ(H), R=ρ{2}(G), and the nonnegative vertical, projection, partition-additivity, and subset defects v, pi, di, δi,
4|D|−k(3γ(G)−R) = v+3Σi(pi+ di)+Σiδi. (6)

Equality therefore requires simultaneous row-wise cell equality, injective minimum column projections, domination additivity across every Li, and terminal odd-clique structure.

7 · A closed hierarchy and an orthogonal bridge

All additive integer-packing levels can be derived exactly: (3m)γ≤m|S|+ρ{2m} and (3m+2)γ≤(m+1)|S|+ ρ{2m+1}. The same formal minimax point saturates every level, while the five-vertex witness realizes ρ{k}=⌊3k/2⌋ and γ{k}=⌈3k/2⌉. This closes the additive hierarchy as a route past 0.5643.

A different construction tensors nonzero fractional packings p,q. With totals P,Q and

κ=maxu,v[ qvp(N[u])+ puq(N[v])− puqv],   γ(G□H)≥PQ/κ. (7)

For regular factors this gives |G||H|/(r+ s+1). The concentrated optimal packing of P4 shows that totals alone cannot control κ.

8 · Formal fibre incidence

Write Γ=γ(G), r=ρ(G), and R=ρ{2}(G). For a complete peeling of each Li, let ℓi be its number of steps and ri the ordinary packing number of its terminal remainder. The exact defects imply

4Σi [ℓi+(r−ri)] ≤ γ(H)(Γ+4r−3R) − v + Σ(pi+di) + 3Σδi.(8)

At the formal Steiner obstruction, 3R=Γ+4r and all other defects vanish. Every fibre set is therefore already terminal, contains a maximum ordinary packing, and has exactly 3r−R singleton atoms and R−2r triangle atoms. Its minimum complement dominator is anticomplete to the fibre set.

The occupied-cell and vertical-cell matrices then have the same row and column margins, so their difference decomposes into alternating cycles. Each row is a disjoint minimum exchange of partition centers in H. These cardinality conditions are not sufficient: an external private target must have a unique coordinate hole in its cell, while a self-private target only forces a nonempty hole set covered by its inside-cell point. A checked C4-based skeleton satisfies all margin and exchange conditions but misses a product vertex, isolating this coordinate-hole condition.

9 · External private targets and hole packings

External-private theorem. In a fully tight atomic column, every member x of the minimum complement dominator has an external private target. If x were self-private only, optimality of the supported 2-packing would produce a saturated witness w∈N[x]. The witness extends through its K1 or K3 atom to a minimum fibre dominator. Replacing x by w makes the two dominators overlap and gives a dominating set of size γ(G)−1.

Fix a product row y. Let J index columns choosing y as an external private target, let P be their singleton coordinate holes, and put e=|Ay|− |Iy|. Replacing the relevant partition centers by the row points and a minimum dominator of P gives

|J|−γH(P)≤ e,   |J|≤ρ(H)+2e. (9)

At zero row slack, P is a two-packing. Summation currently gives only the order-dependent condition |D|≤|V(G)|ρ(H). Symmetric external privacy does not repair this: the perfect code {(i,2i):i∈ℤ5} in C5□C5 cycles every opposite rectangle corner through the next codeword.

10 · Two orthogonal LP boundaries

Rank-one fractional tensors

Every certificate in (7) obeys the exact ceiling

PQ/κ ≤ min{ |H|γf(G), |G|γf(H)}. (10)

Against P4, optimal and fixed-tolerance near-optimal packings have κ arbitrarily close to one, independently of the integrality gap. A connected split-graph family puts every rank-one certificate below Steiner. Deliberately diffuse suboptimal packings can still help on classes: the hard-only m=8,k=4 split graph paired with the uniform one-third packing on P4 gives γ(G□P4)≥8, a normalized factor 4/5.

Square-clique covers

Let σ(G) be the fractional vertex clique-cover number of G2. Strong-product multiplicativity and a weighted slice count give

γ(G□H) ≥ max{σ(G)γ(H), γ(G)σ(H)}.(11)

If G2 is perfect and each of its cliques lies in a closed neighborhood of G, then σ(G)=γ(G) and Vizing holds against every H. Forests satisfy both conditions. Universally the relaxation has unbounded loss: for Gm=L(K2m+1) and H=P4, its product-square clique cover is at most four while γ(Gm)γ(P4)=2m.

11 · Bidirectional blocker lift

For nonnegative weights w on K, let τK(w) be the minimum weighted cost of an integral dominating set. Maximize

ΣgτH (ag,·)+ ΣhτG (b·,h) (12)

over nonnegative arrays satisfying Σg∈N[u] ag,v+ Σh∈N[v] bu,h≤1. Call the optimum Λ(G,H). Projecting a product dominator through every closed neighborhood proves

γ(G□H)≥Λ(G,H)≤ γ(G)γ(H).(13)

Unit column weights on the two endpoints of P4 give Λ(G,P4)=2γ(G) for every G. The lift is not universally stronger: on vertex-transitive factors of degrees r,s, automorphism averaging gives exactly

Λ=max{|G|γ(H)/(r+1), |H|γ(G)/(s+1)}. (14)

For two copies of L(K2m+1), the normalized value is (2m+1)/(4m−1)→1/2. Pure nonseparable fractional packing is separately blocked by the connected split graph against P4.

The lift also has a precise fractional complement. For an ordinary maximum two-packing in G and a fractional packing q on H, with total Q, define ΔH (q) as the minimum sum of 1−q(N[v]) over a dominating set of H. An explicit feasible blocker solution gives

Λ(G,H)≥Qγ(G)+ ρ(G)ΔH(q). (15)

Double counting gives the exact correction ΔH(q)= γ(H)−Q−ΩH(q)≤ γ(H)−Q. The canonical half-2-packing can therefore only meet Steiner at its absolute ceiling; it can never improve it. Optimizing the valid slice still fails: exact C5/split mixtures approaching the formal ratios keep its normalized value at (1273−115√73)/576≈0.504235.

The independently capped fractional-packing repair also fails. The augmented split graph has an exact three-piece capped profile and a uniform normalized hard-spike error at most 2/γ. An asymmetric C5/L(K7) additive family keeps both anchored arms and every pair of caps below Steiner. Safe zero-weight ports connect the family while preserving γ, ρ and ρ{2}, so connectedness does not repair the no-go.

12 · Labelled private-corner dynamics

Row hole two-packings tensor with every factor packing, but a common-crown construction concentrates all private incidences in one closed neighborhood. Two-sided external privacy does force a genuinely labelled object. Each dominating point has a horizontal and vertical private neighbor; the opposite corner is owned by another point of the dominating set. Choosing one owner per corner creates a directed cycle whose every step changes one coordinate by an edge and the other by distance exactly two.

At formal equality, escape indegrees are bounded by the row-hole packing numbers and every arc has a red-diagonal/blue-cross-zero cell-label pattern. The perfect code in C5□C5 realizes a directed 5-cycle, so cycle existence and bounded indegree alone do not give a density gain.

13 · Combined packing/blocker lift and exact STOP

Let a genuine fractional product packing W share each local capacity unit with both blocker arrays. Maximizing ΣW+ΣτH(a)+ΣτG(b) under the shared constraint defines Ξ(G,H), and the same dominating-set count proves γ(G□H)≥Ξ(G,H). Its exact dual is a fractional product dominator that simultaneously routes owner-indexed probability marginals of integral factor dominators.

For vertex-transitive factors with closed-neighborhood sizes RG,RH, put κG=γ(G)RG/|G| and similarly for H. Exact averaging gives the normalized value

max{1/κG, 1/κH, RGRH/ [κGκH(RG+RH−1)]}. (16)

Bollobás–Janson–Riordan translate coverings yield affinely spanning sets in elementary abelian 2-groups with κ→∞. Their Cayley graphs are connected and vertex-transitive. Taking the two neighborhood scales sufficiently different sends all three terms above to zero. Hence Ξ, and therefore the full blocker alone, has no positive universal normalized factor.

14 · Typed fibre-set successor

For actual row label sets Ag, let Vg be the union of labels imported from open neighboring rows. Product domination is exactly H∖Vg⊆ NH[Ag]. Keeping only the necessary cardinality inequality in every row and column defines a strict typed relaxation Θ.

The row masses form an integer γ(H)-dominating function on G. More directly, every genuinely missed row subset can be repaired at total cost at most the original label count, giving

Θ≥γ(G□H)/2 ≥(c/2)γ(G)γ(H). (17)

The relaxation is strict on (K2⊔K1)□(K2⊔K1): four swapped-component labels pass both cardinality systems, while the product domination number is five. The live information is not a better repair factor but the correlated labels retained by the following exact inequalities.

15 · Partial-cover profile and Cayley calibration

Define uK(t) as the least number of vertices left uncovered by at most t closed neighborhoods. Every typed incidence set, with row masses ag and column masses bh, satisfies

ΣguH(ag) +ΣhuG(bh)≤|G||H|. (18)

The proof has an exact defect identity: its three terms are import-profile excess, partial-cover suboptimality, and isolated induced fibres. For a d-regular first factor,

Θ(G,H)≥|G|(t+1)uH(t) / [uH(t)+d(t+1)]. (19)

A robust adaptation of the Newman/Bollobás–Janson–Riordan random-translate argument constructs connected Cayley graphs on F2m with t=(n/k)(log k−10 log log k)+O(1), u(t)>(log k)6, and γ∼(n/k)log k. Equation (19) beats γ(G)γ(H) for every pair of growing graphs in this family, at arbitrary relative scales. Thus the labels in Θ decisively defeat the Cayley construction that sends Ξ to zero. This robust profile statement is a new adaptation, not a theorem stated verbatim in the cited paper.

16 · Correlated fractional repair energy

For factor fractional packings q,p, totals Q,P, and actual remaining row and column targets Cg,Dh, define

Z=Σ(g,h)∈A(1−qg)(1−ph),   EH=Σgqg [γH(Cg)−p(Cg)], (20)

Define αH=Σgqg [|Ag|−γH(Cg)] and αG symmetrically. Exact double counting gives

|A|≥PQ+Z+ max{EH+αH,EG+αG}. (21)

Each repair gap splits into unsaturated repair owners and positive-packing targets covered more than once. For half of an integral 2-packing every nonzero gap costs at least one half. The cap-only corollary has denominator s+t−st, but the formal-ratio counterfamily still bounds it by (−247+37√73)/132≈0.523698<c. The energy terms are the live content.

On a terminal K1/K3 equality set, triangle parity gives Z+EH≥τQ/2. This is rigorous but yields only (10−√73)/9≈0.16178 at the formal ratios. Provider reuse across different fibre-indexed triangle systems is the remaining obstruction.

17 · Isolation-to-escape density

Let IG count selected points isolated in their fixed-label G-fibres. Let ΩG count repeated same-label horizontal imports and XH count imported labels redundantly covered inside their arrival row. Points lacking a horizontal external-private neighbor satisfy

|BadG|≤2ΩG+XH. (22)

Applying this in both orientations, the number T of fibre-isolated points with external private neighbors in both directions obeys

|T|≥[IG+IH−|D| −2ΩG−2ΩH−XG−XH]+. (23)

Every such point carries a labelled mixed (1,2) or (2,1) corner escape obligation. The bound is exact on the C5□C5 5-cycle and K2□P3 2-cycle.

18 · Overlap and multiplicity ledger

Let C and D0 be the no-horizontal and no-vertical open-import regions. Put I=A∩C∩D0, W=(C∩D0)\A, J=(G□H)\(C∪D0), and w(g,h)=qgph. Then K=w(A\I)+w(J) is the exact overlap tax, and

|A|=PQ+Z+EH+EG +αH+αG−K+w(W). (24)

If m(z) counts all owners, t(z) only records existence in the self, horizontal, and vertical channels, R=Σw(z)[m(z)−t(z)], and Δ is the factor-packing load slack at selected owners, then

2(|A|−PQ−Z)=EH+EG +αH+αG+R+Δ. (25)

The two one-axis residuals are nonnegative and sum exactly to R+Δ. Minimum dominators of K2□K2 and P3□P3 make the corrected directional maximum sharp.

19 · Two complete no-go classifications

Escape realization. Every finite bipartite graph F without isolates occurs as a zero-defect escape graph. If H is its one-subdivision with parts S,T, then D={(0,s):s∈S}∪{(1,t):t∈T} is a minimum perfect code in K2□H.

Stars give cyclic fraction 2/(k+1)→0 and paths give arbitrarily long in-trees. Generic cycle-density, bounded-return, injective-owner, and additive escape-credit arguments therefore fail even when all present defects vanish.

Actual factor calibration. There are graphs K with γ(K)→∞, ρ(K)=1, and ρ{2}(K)=2. Mixing these with isolated vertices and C5 components in domination-mass fractions c=(5+√73)/24, (47−5√73)/24, and (√73−7)/6 makes ρ/γ→(11−√73)/8 and ρ{2}/(2γ)→(13−√73)/12.

The dense components follow from elementary G(n,½) union bounds: simultaneously every pair and triple lies in a closed neighborhood, while no bounded-size set dominates. Hence Steiner’s formal optimizer is the limit of actual graphs; factor-invariant reoptimization cannot exclude it.

20 · Indexed provider reuse obstruction

For one row, join an indexed terminal demand i to a selected coordinate a when a∈N[yi]\πi. Unavoidable reuse is exactly the Hall deficiency |I|−ν(P)=maxJ⊆I(|J|−|N(J)|).

An explicit actual dominator in C5□Hm has m identical exact terminal K3 fibres, globally optimal canonical 2-packings, and minimum size-preserving provider-row exchanges, yet the same selected point uniquely serves all m demands. The construction pays linear projection, additivity, subset, and vertical defects; the four-region tax is zero for each natural indexed packing. Hence indexed atoms or overlap tax alone do not control cross-index reuse.

b²+2b(b−a)=(13−√73)/24≈0.18567<c. (26)

At least eighteen independently additive triangle charges are needed merely to cross Steiner’s constant. A viable theorem must couple outgoing terminal indices to incoming private-hole two-packings and charge Hall deficiency to the full exact fibre-defect budget.

21 · Adaptive providers and labelled cell cycles

Adaptive row-provider theorem. For every row g, let Ig={i:g∈Li} and let Ag be its selected H-coordinates. There is an injection μg:Ig→Ag such that μg(i)∉πi and NH[μg(i)]∩πi≠∅.

Hall’s condition follows from γH(⋃i∈Igπi)=|Ig|: too few neighboring providers for J⊆Ig, together with the retained centers for Ig\J, would give a smaller dominator.

Globally the matching leaves exactly v=|D|−Σi|Li| selected points unused. At v=0 and |Di|=|Li|, its genuine cell transitions form a loopless Eulerian digraph and hence decompose into labelled directed cycles. Under the full external-private column hypotheses, they transfer injectively to singleton holes and give the local open-neighborhood cut

Σg∈U|Ig| ≤ρ(H)|NopenG(U)| +2Σx∈NopenG(U)ex. (27)

Adaptivity is essential. In C5□Hm, the matching chooses ti with distinct provider ai, while the pre-prescribed si demands still all require a0. The triangle-only scalar consequence is weaker than the existing all-incidence row-hole inequality, so no new constant follows.
