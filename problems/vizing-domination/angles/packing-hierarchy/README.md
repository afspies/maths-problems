# The full additive integer-packing hierarchy

## Status

**General inequalities proved; hierarchy obstruction proved.** Every
inequality obtained by additively combining Steiner's two base subset bounds
with integer packing superadditivity is saturated by the same formal
minimax obstruction. A five-vertex graph supplies an actual local equality
gadget for all levels.

## General hierarchy

Write

`g=γ_G(S)`, `s=|S|`, `r=ρ_G(S)`, and `R_k=ρ^{\{k\}}(G)`.

For every integer `m≥1`,

`3m g ≤ m s+R_{2m}`.                                          (E_m)

For every integer `m≥0`,

`(3m+2)g ≤ (m+1)s+R_{2m+1}`.                                  (O_m)

For `(E_m)`, add `m` copies of Steiner's
`3g≤s+R_2` and use `R_{2m}≥mR_2`. For `(O_m)`, also add
`2g≤s+r` and use `R_{2m+1}≥mR_2+r`. These packing inequalities follow by
adding optimal nonnegative integer packing functions; the indicator of a
maximum two-packing contained in `S` supplies the final 1-packing.

The exact slacks are

```text
m(s+R_2-3g) + (R_{2m}-mR_2),

m(s+R_2-3g) + (s+r-2g) + (R_{2m+1}-mR_2-r).
```

Thus any strict improvement from this hierarchy must force a base subset
slack or a packing-superadditivity slack.

## Product bounds

Substitution into Steiner's fibre count gives

`γ(G□H) ≥ ((3mγ(G)-R_{2m}(G))/(4m))γ(H)`                    (PE_m)

and

`γ(G□H) ≥ (((3m+2)γ(G)-R_{2m+1}(G))/(4m+3))γ(H)`.           (PO_m)

The cases `m=1` and `m=0,1` recover the known `R_2`, ordinary packing, and
corrected `R_3` bounds.

## Exact relaxed obstruction at every level

Let

`c=(5+√73)/24`, `a=2-3c`, and `b=3/2-2c`.

At the formal normalized obstruction set `γ(G)=1` and

`R_{2m}=2mb`, `R_{2m+1}=2mb+a`.

For subset data take `g=1-c`, `s=c`, and `r=a`. Both base subset slacks and
every packing-additivity slack vanish. Every hierarchy product bound equals
`c`. The Hou--Lu mixed terms at even levels also equal `c`; at odd levels
they are smaller because `(2mb+a)/(2m+1)<b`.

Therefore the entire cone generated by the two base subset inequalities and
packing superadditivity has relaxed minimax exactly `c`. No number of higher
`k` parameters can improve 0.5643 without a relation outside this cone.

## Actual all-level equality gadget

Let `Q` have edges

`01,03,04,12,14,23`

and take `S={2,3,4}`. Then

`g=2`, `s=3`, `r=R_1=1`, `R_2=3`, and

`R_k=⌊3k/2⌋` for every `k≥0`.

The lower bound is attained by balanced weight on vertices `2,3,4`.
For the upper bound, add the packing constraints on `N[0]`, `N[1]`, and
`N[2]` and divide by two; this bounds total weight by `3k/2` (with an extra
nonnegative coefficient on one variable). Integrality gives the floor.

Hence

`R_{2m}=mR_2` and `R_{2m+1}=mR_2+R_1`

for every `m`, and both base subset inequalities—and therefore the entire
hierarchy—are equalities.

The domination side is simultaneously tight:

`γ^{\{k\}}(Q)=⌈3k/2⌉`.

Weak duality against the 2-packing of weight three gives the lower bound.
For `k=2m`, weights `(m,m,0,m,0)` are a `k`-dominating function of total
`3m`; for `k=2m+1`, weights `(m,m,0,m+1,1)` have total `3m+2`. Thus even the
full `k`-domination/Hou--Lu hierarchy has no hidden slack in this gadget.

More generally, weak duality gives

`γ^{\{q\}}(G)≥q·sup_k(R_k/k)`.

At the formal obstruction the supremum is `b`, attained at every even level,
so the domination hierarchy reproduces the existing mixed term
`a+b(1-a)=c`.

The gadget does not realize the irrational Steiner ratios. It proves that
the additive packing and domination defects cannot be forced positive by a
purely local hierarchy argument, while the normalized construction proves
the exact formal minimax obstruction.

## Verdict

**PIVOT from the additive packing hierarchy.** A viable next lemma must couple
different fibre sets, impose non-additive structure on `R_k`, or use a
product-side quantity outside this hierarchy.
