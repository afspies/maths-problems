Abstract The current general Hausdorff benchmark in R⁴ is above 3.059, the current maximal-function benchmark is the distinct 3.0543 result, and the sticky Hausdorff benchmark is 13/4. We reconstructed the sticky induction, found and repaired a constants defect in its printed bookkeeping, and built an exact ruled-quadric stress test. No new Kakeya dimension bound is claimed.

1 · Verified bookkeeping

Proposition 1. If N is minimal with 4/N < ε0, then ε0/(4+ε0) ≤ 1/N < ε0/4. The two published Section 6 comparisons using ε0/4 on the opposite side do not follow from the stated choices. Replacing it by the safe lower bound ε0/5 and shrinking the auxiliary losses repairs the induction budget.

2 · Ruled-quadric stress test

Proposition 2. The quadric x1²+x2²− x3²−x4²=1 contains three concurrent rational lines whose normalized squared trilinear wedge is 1/50. Thus a ruled quadratic carrier need not be 2-plany even locally.

The linear identification with SL2(R) makes the ruling exact. The true Convex-Wolff obstruction is the randomly thinned net followed by δ−1/2 moved copies; the raw full net itself fails Convex Wolff. A degree-2 polynomial Wolff test detects each thinned copy by δ−1/2.

4 · Conditional exponent bridge

Target. A multiscale trilinear gain |U| ≥ δ3/4−c+o(1), for some 0<c<1/12, would leave the weakly plany gain ρ−(1/12−c) positive and imply dimH K ≥ 13/4+c.

This target is not proved. The current one-scale trilinear estimate has no factor capable of producing δ−c, and Proposition 3.12 contains no near-equality classification or cross-scale algebraic coherence. The soundness review also rejected combining independently selected balanced refinements without a common-mass lemma or ignoring model-selection losses.
