Verified bookkeeping

Proposition 1. If N is minimal with 4/N<ε0, then ε0/(4+ε0)≤1/N<ε0/4. The published comparisons using ε0/4 on the other side do not follow. The lower bound ε0/5, a smaller ε1, and a shrunk η-hierarchy repair the exponent budget.

For ε0=1/10, exact arithmetic gives N=41. The printed multiplicity exponent also needs the sign −σ4+2η. Two elementary admissibility fixes are recorded: impose η0≤1/N for the intermediate-scale interval, and weaken the available exponent-one two-ends condition to an exponent strictly below one before applying the planebrush theorem.

Ruled-quadric stress test

Proposition 2. The quadric x1²+x2²− x3²−x4²=1 contains three concurrent rational lines with normalized squared trilinear wedge 1/50. Thus a ruled quadratic carrier need not be 2-plany even locally.

The linear identification with SL2(R) verifies the ruling exactly. The Convex-Wolff obstruction is the thinned line net followed by δ−1/2 moved copies; the raw full net itself fails Convex Wolff. A degree-two polynomial test detects each thinned copy by δ−1/2.
