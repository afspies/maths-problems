Four-dimensional Kakeya: extraction frontier

Exact union, covering, and quadratic-degeneracy theorems

partial-results · bridge B go · 24 July 2026 · problems/kakeya-r4

Abstract. The full R⁴ Kakeya conjecture remains open. Harmonically transverse ruled quadratic stacks have dense shaded union at least λ²/log(1/δ). A fixed all-scale continuum stack satisfies a scale-sensitive incidence inequality, giving Hausdorff dimension four for that structured subclass. Exact countermodels show why small union does not itself extract such carriers, and a quadratic-sublevel theorem now handles linearly rank-two-separated parabolic coefficient paths. The affine-rotating rank-one path has an endpoint L3/2 collar bound and cubic SSI, giving Hausdorff dimension four for every fixed uniformly swept stack in that class. Full-tree subpolynomial ancestry is false, but a one-shot cover partition eliminates transverse wall mass after charging cover cost. No new general or sticky dimension bound is claimed.

Main theorem

Harmonic transverse-stack theorem. Let M≤Cδ−1 uniformly regular ruled quadric patches carry dense λ-shadings. If every double-overlap region satisfies

|∇Pi∧∇Pj| ≥c|i−j|/M,

then

|⋃Ui| ≥ cλ²Mδ / (1+MδHM−1). For M≈δ−1, this is cλ²/log(2+M).

Proof mechanism

Reach and the dense line sweep give |Ui|≥cλδ. Coarea on the rank-two locus, together with Crofton–Bézout, gives

|Ui∩Uj| ≤Cδ²M/|i−j|.

The pairwise losses sum to the harmonic number. The exact second moment then proves the displayed union bound. A weighted sparse form is also proved.

Fixed-stack Hausdorff theorem

Scale-sensitive incidence criterion. For one fixed measurable segment family, let A(V) be the longitudinal incidence mass lying in a measurable set V. If for every small dyadic r,

|NCr(V)| ≥ cA(V)²/L(r),

and Σr≤r₀r4−sL(r)→0 for every s<4, then Hs(K)=∞ and dimHK=4.

A fixed continuum harmonic stack supplies L(r)=1+log(1/r). More generally, retaining a fraction b(r)=ro(1) of every cover group’s incidence mass in one admissible chart is sufficient. This cover-by-cover qualifier is essential: unrelated stacks extracted from the whole union at each scale do not control arbitrary Hausdorff covers.

Explicit nonvacuous family

Put

q0=x1²+x2²−x3²−x4²,   q1=2x1x3+x3²,   Ps=q0+sq1−1.

Every member has split signature and inherits the exact SL2(R) line sweep. The combined carrier/direction chart has rational seed determinant 3/16, while

∇Ps∧∇Ps′ =(s′−s)∇q0∧∇q1.

Compact restriction yields the theorem’s uniform hypotheses. Fixed all-scale families covered by finitely many such direction charts have Hausdorff dimension four. This smooth explicit sweep actually has positive measure locally by the rank-four inverse function theorem; it is a nonvacuity model, not a delicate measure-zero construction.

Why small union does not extract carriers

If A is total shaded incidence and V is union volume, the multiplicity function satisfies ∫m²≥A²/V, and a high-multiplicity level carries a fixed fraction of A. This output contains no polynomial or ruling.

An exact grid of M≈δ−1 transverse hyperplane slabs gives distributed overlap q≈Mδ≈1: every tube crosses every slab for length Θ(δ), but no tube has assigned overlap λ≫δ with any slab. A valid extraction theorem must therefore produce assigned overlap, excess q−Mδ, or a geometric rule excluding ordinary crossings.

Inverse carrier energy. Once M genuine quadratic patches with |Ui|≈λδ are supplied,

|⋃Ui| ≥ cM²λ²δ² /(Mδ+Σi<jmin(δ,δ²/κij)).

Thus a small carrier union forces large inverse-normal-Jacobian energy. Classifying that energy is the missing theorem.

Degenerate quadratic branch

Regular irreducible nonlinear Hessian rank at most two, and regular central rank three, are pointwise 2-plany. Affine or reducible hyperplanes and conical spines are separate outputs. The genuine smooth ruled nonlinear exception is the indefinite rank-three paraboloid.

For Ps=z−y1y2−sy3²,

|∇Ps∧∇Pt| =2|s−t||x3||∇Ps∧e3|.

Away from |x3|≤ρ the transverse union estimate loses a real factor ρ; inside the slab the directions enter a weakly 2-plany alternative. This is an exact stress model, not yet a scale-loss-free rigidity theorem.

Rank-two-separated parabolic stacks

Quadratic-sublevel stack theorem. For graph quadrics Ps(y,z)=z−yTAsy−ℓ(y), assume σ2(As−At)≥c|s−t|. Then at M≈δ−1,

|⋃Ui| ≥ cλ²/log²(1/δ).

The fixed continuum family satisfies |NCr(V)|≥cA(V)²/log²(1/r), and hence has Hausdorff dimension four.

This applies even when carrier normals coincide on a locus. The exact path As=diag((1+s)²,−1,(1+s)²) has ruled-sweep and direction-chart determinants of absolute value 4.

Exact complete cliques of pair differences cℓ² lie in one common-square pencil. The rotating moment path A(s)=∫0s(1,t,0)(1,t,0)Tdt has rank-one derivative but only cubic second-singular-value separation. It is the next two-scale obstruction.

Parent ancestry crosses the catalog baseline

For a degree-D parent wall and one line, the quantitatively transverse sublevel length is at most CDr/α. If descendant incidences are unioned or uniquely assigned under only K parent polynomials, their total transverse error is CKDr/α—independent of descendant count.

An additive Hausdorff criterion allows A(V)≤Σν≤J(r)Aν(V)+e(r). Summable e(r) and Σr4−sJ(r)²L0(r)→0 imply Hausdorff dimension four, provided the Aν are genuine fixed or cell-averaged continuum chart functionals satisfying SSI. Organizing the nontransverse-or-singular remainder into those charts is still open.

Endpoint affine-rotating rank-one stacks

Endpoint collar theorem. For As=A0+ ∫0s(p+tq)(p+tq)Tdt, with p,q linearly independent, the varying graph height at a=p·y, b=q·y is

ha,b(s)=a²s+ab s²+b²s³/3,   h′(s)=(a+bs)².

The normalized full-collar multiplicity obeys ∫mr3/2≤C log(2/r). Hence every jointly measurable shading of a fixed uniformly swept stack satisfies

|NCr(V)|≥cA(V)³/log²(2/r),

and the swept set has Hausdorff dimension four.

At the critical point, h(s)−h(s0)=b²(s−s0)³/3. The fixed-b L3/2 cost is |b|−1log(2+b²/r), while the critical a-wedge has width O(|b|); the singular factors cancel. A cubic SSI uses the Hausdorff scale sum Σr(4−s)/2L(r)1/2, which converges at every s<4. Pairwise second moments see only dimension 11/3.

One-shot cover partition

Recursive dyadic hyperplane bisection to scale r makes one transverse line cross Θ(r−1) distinct parents. Any polynomial compressing those exact crossings has degree Ω(r−1), so full-tree subpolynomial ancestry is false.

For one cover group with nk radius-rk balls, take one degree Dk≈nk1/4 partition. If ck=nkrks, its normalized transverse error is

ek≤Cαk−1 ck1/4 rk(4−s)/4.

Under bounded proposed s-cost and αk≥rkε, ε<(4−s)/4, Hölder makes this tail vanish. The cellular branch and the tangent/singular high-degree wall remain open.
