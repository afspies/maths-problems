Four-dimensional Kakeya: degree-two carriers

A harmonic union theorem for transverse stacks of ruled quadrics

partial-results · bridge B go · 24 July 2026 · problems/kakeya-r4

Abstract. The full R⁴ Kakeya conjecture remains open. This campaign proves a semialgebraic union theorem for an explicit infinite family of ruled quadratic obstructions. Harmonically transverse stacks have dense shaded union at least λ²/log(1/δ), yielding full Minkowski dimension for a structured subclass. No new general, sticky, or Hausdorff bound is claimed.

Main theorem

Harmonic transverse-stack theorem. Let M≤Cδ−1 uniformly regular ruled quadric patches carry dense λ-shadings. If every double-overlap region satisfies

|∇Pi∧∇Pj| ≥c|i−j|/M,

then

|⋃Ui| ≥ cλ²Mδ / (1+MδHM−1). For M≈δ−1, this is cλ²/log(2+M).

Proof mechanism

Reach and the dense line sweep give |Ui|≥cλδ. Coarea on the rank-two locus, together with Crofton–Bézout, gives

|Ui∩Uj| ≤Cδ²M/|i−j|.

The pairwise losses sum to the harmonic number. The exact second moment then proves the displayed union bound. A weighted sparse form is also proved, but it is not yet a Hausdorff covering theorem.

Explicit nonvacuous family

Put

q0=x1²+x2²−x3²−x4²,   q1=2x1x3+x3²,   Ps=q0+sq1−1.

Every member has split signature and inherits the exact SL2(R) line sweep. The combined carrier/direction chart has rational seed determinant 3/16, while

∇Ps∧∇Ps′ =(s′−s)∇q0∧∇q1.

Compact restriction yields the theorem’s uniform hypotheses. Dense Kakeya discretizations covered by finitely many such direction charts have full lower and upper Minkowski dimension four.
