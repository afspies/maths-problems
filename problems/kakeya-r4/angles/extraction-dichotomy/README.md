# Carrier-extraction dichotomy

## Status

The exact accounting and no-go countermodel are proved in
`../../results/carrier-extraction-accounting.md`. Small union alone extracts
a high-multiplicity incidence level, not quadratic carriers. A constant
distributed catalog overlap is vacuous when `M≈delta^-1`, because ordinary
transverse crossings already contribute `Mdelta≈1`.

## Correct target

A useful four-dimensional extraction theorem must start from a
high-multiplicity incidence level and output one of:

1. assigned tubes with overlap `lambda≫delta` on
   `delta^(-1+o(1))` ruled carriers;
2. an excess distributed overlap `q-Mdelta` large enough to survive the
   fourth-power layer-cake loss;
3. failure of quadratic grain capture, with a quantitative alternative that
   advances the induction; or
4. a large low-Jacobian carrier cluster classified into a coherent pencil,
   a 2-plany lower-rank branch, a conical-spine bush, or a rank-three
   parabolic family.

The result note proves the exact entropy required in outputs 1--2 and an
inverse tangency-energy theorem after carriers have been extracted.

## Why this is the full-conjecture bottleneck

The harmonic transverse-stack theorem handles coherently ordered
well-conditioned carriers. The degenerate classification handles
irreducible nonlinear rank-at-most-two and central rank-three models
pointwise, separates hyperplanes, and gives an exact
transverse-versus-plany result for one rank-three parabolic pencil with a
charged `rho^-1` transverse loss.

What is not known is a theorem that converts local polynomial-partition wall
pieces into those outputs with:

- degree two rather than the partition degree;
- only `delta^(-1+o(1))` global carrier entropy;
- positive assigned overlap rather than baseline crossings;
- a common direction chart and retained shading mass;
- persistence through the scales used in the Hausdorff cover.

Assuming this package and then applying the checked-in inner/outer theorems
would be circular. Proving it is the live full-conjecture bridge.
