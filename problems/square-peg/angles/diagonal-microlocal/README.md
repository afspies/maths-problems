# Persistent diagonal locality

## Exact unrestricted target

Normalize the enclosed area to \(\pi\) and work over
\(\mathbb F_2\).  For a planar-null Jordan trace \(C\), choose a compactly
supported area-preserving homeomorphism
\(\phi\) with \(\phi(C_0)=C\).  Put
\[
 F_C=K(\phi\times\phi)F_{C_0}.
\]
For \(\theta\in(0,\pi)\), Asano--Ike define a critical value
\[
 a_0=-a(\theta,C),\qquad0<a(\theta,C)<\pi,
\]
from the filtered lift of the generator
\(v\in H^1(\operatorname{End}(F_C))\).

Remark 4.2 says it is enough to prove
\[
 R\Gamma\!\left(
 \rho^{-1}(\Delta_C);
 \mu hom(F_C,T_{a_0}R_\theta F_C)
 \big|_{\rho^{-1}(\Delta_C)}
 \right)\simeq0.                                          \tag{1}
\]
The inverse image \(\rho^{-1}(\Delta_C)\) includes every Tamarkin
coordinate \(t\in\mathbb R/\pi\mathbb Z\) and every \(\tau>0\).
Equation (1) is a derived-global-sections statement, not pointwise
microsupport disjointness.

If \(C\) has no \(\theta\)-rectangle, then
\[
 (C\times C)\cap R_\theta(C\times C)=\Delta_C.
\]
The critical microstalk is then entirely supported over the diagonal, and
criticality makes the global sections non-zero.  Thus (1) contradicts the
absence of a rectangle.

## Conjugated square formulation

Set
\[
 L=C_0\times C_0,\qquad
 \Phi=\phi\times\phi,\qquad
 Q=\Phi^{-1}R_{\pi/2}\Phi.
\]
Let \(F_0\) be the standard torus quantization.  The exact theorem needed
for unrestricted Square Peg is:

> **Persistent diagonal locality.**  If \(L\cap Q(L)=\Delta_0\), then for
> every \(a\notin\pi\mathbb Z\),
> \[
> R\Gamma\!\left(
> \rho^{-1}(\Delta_0);
> \mu hom(F_0,T_aQF_0)|_{\rho^{-1}(\Delta_0)}
> \right)\simeq0.                                         \tag{2}
> \]

This is not being asserted as proved.  It is the remaining theorem.

## What the local geometry does prove

For smooth conservative approximants \(\phi_n\to\phi\), the maps and
inverses are uniformly equicontinuous.  With
\(\Phi_n=\phi_n\times\phi_n\), the conjugated paths
\[
 Q_{n,t}=\Phi_n^{-1}R_t\Phi_n
\]
fix the full diagonal pointwise.  Their Hamiltonians are
\[
 H_n=H\circ\Phi_n,\qquad H(z,w)=|z-w|^2/4,
\]
so \(H_n\) vanishes on \(\Delta_0\) and its oscillation on shrinking
diagonal tubes tends uniformly to zero.  Since \(R_t\) preserves the
distance between its two planar inputs, trajectories beginning in a
sufficiently small tube remain in a slightly larger tube.

These facts justify a small local Hofer/interleaving estimate.  They do
not prove (2).

## Why the germ argument stops

The neighborhood on which the interleaving bound is small shrinks with
the requested error.  There is no fixed neighborhood on which
\(QF_0\) equals \(F_0\).  Moreover:

- the action integrand contains \(\lambda(X_{H_n})\), which is sensitive
  to derivatives even when \(H_n\) has small amplitude;
- small interleaving distance controls barcode persistence, not the
  location of every individual action branch;
- \(\mu hom\) does not commute with metric limits;
- global nondegeneracy for limit-constructible Tamarkin objects does not
  imply nondegeneracy of the pro-germ obtained from neighborhoods
  shrinking to \(\Delta_0\).

The fixed-vertex twists in
`../../results/shrinking-square-action-injection.md` show that collapsing
off-diagonal generators can retain arbitrary actions under exactly this
geometric setup.

## The bar-level strengthening to prove

A usable approximation-stable version of (2) is:

> For every \(\eta>0\), let \(\Phi_n\to\Phi\) be smooth conservative
> approximations.  Every local Floer/persistence bar generated by
> intersections tending to \(\Delta_0\) and having length at least
> \(\eta\) has both endpoints within \(o(1)\) of \(\pi\mathbb Z\).

Individual generators may have arbitrary limiting actions.  The theorem
must pair all interior-action collapsing generators into bars whose
lengths tend to zero.  Such a pairing would eliminate their contribution
to the critical global diagonal cohomology and prove (2).

## Exact null-spiral local model

Finite smooth truncations of the null double spiral have clean diagonal
Floer cohomology \(H^*(S^1;\mathbb F_2)\) entirely at action
\(0\pmod\pi\), so every interior diagonal translate vanishes.  In the wild
limit, however, the common diagonal phase winds through the full action
circle while the two boundary sheets approach with gap
\[
 \delta(\theta)=\frac{\pi}{2\theta}+O(\theta^{-2}).
\]
Thus the reduced limiting microsupport contains the whole \(t\)-circle
over the collapsed prime end, even though every finite diagonal complex
vanishes there.

The full calculation and corrected action-retaining extension model are in
`../../results/null-spiral-microlocal-eye.md`.  The original model collapsed
the action circle and incorrectly inferred a translated morphism from a
common boundary stalk.  With the full action circle retained, the
\(!\)-versus-\(*\) cone is a local system with \(\tau_t=0\) and vanishes in
the Tamarkin quotient.  The deep eye is arbitrarily torsion.

The genuine missing datum is the telescope of continuation maps.  One must
show that its cap/limiting cone cannot leave an exact-action Milnor boundary
term after restricting \(\mu hom\) to the collapsed diagonal.

## Square symmetry does not supply the pairing

At the square angle,
\[
 Q^2=R_\pi,
\]
and \(R_\pi\) swaps the two factors.  On the standard quantization,
\(R_\pi F_0\simeq F_0[1]\).  This exchanges complementary action
filtrations and cohomological degrees; it does not identify a diagonal
complex with a non-trivial shift of itself, pin the critical action to
\(\pi/2\), or construct the required short-bar pairing.

## Why an arbitrarily small diagonal cut-off is still insufficient

The conjugated Hamiltonian
\[
 H^\phi(z,w)=|\phi(z)-\phi(w)|^2/4
\]
vanishes on the diagonal.  Composing it with a cut-off which is the identity
near zero gives a flow with the same shrinking diagonal germ and arbitrarily
small Hofer oscillation.  This does **not** prove (2).

The persistence objects
\[
 V_\varepsilon
 =V_{\rm std}\oplus\mathbb k_{[a_0,a_0+\varepsilon)}
\]
converge to \(V_{\rm std}\) in interleaving distance while retaining a
non-zero microstalk at the fixed endpoint \(a_0\) for every
\(\varepsilon\).  An ordinary action skyscraper has zero one-sided
interleaving distance from zero but non-zero global cohomology.  These are
exact models of the zero-lifetime class that Remark 4.2 must exclude.

Thus a valid cut-off proof additionally needs a \(C^0\)-locality theorem for
completed GKS kernels and a no-ephemeral/derived-Milnor theorem for the
restricted diagonal \(\mu hom\).  See
`../../results/metric-germ-cutoff-no-go.md`.
