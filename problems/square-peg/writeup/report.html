Rectangular pegs from Young-regular Jordan curves

A finite-variation bridge to the Asano–Ike criterion

partial result problems/square-peg · 2026-07-24 · unrestricted conjecture open

Abstract. We combine Asano–Ike’s 2026 smooth-approximation criterion with Boedihardjo–Geng’s embedded polygon interpolation and Young integration. Every Jordan parametrization of finite p-variation, p<2, inscribes every prescribed rectangle. Dini–Young and Antonelli–Young quadratic-diameter conditions reach the critical scale. An explicit critical spiral comb has infinite p-variation for every p<2, yet still gets every rectangle. At p=2, a positive-area ½-Hölder Jordan curve gives incompatible inner and outer embedded primitive limits. More sharply, a planar-null double spiral admits no parameter-aligned C1 approximation with convergent primitives. Its conformal exhaustion concentrates fixed action at zero critical trace capacity, producing an explicit vanishing-width microlocal eye. The unrestricted conjecture remains open.

1 · Main theorem

Theorem. Let c:S1→ℝ2 be a Jordan parametrization of finite p-variation for some 1≤p<2. Then c(S1) inscribes a θ-rectangle for every θ∈(0,π), including a square.
Critical extension. The conclusion also holds if the periodic coordinate moduli satisfy ∫01ωx(r)ωy(r)r−2dr<∞.
Exact critical extension. The conclusion also holds when c is ½-Hölder and Σi,j diam{c(j2−i), c((2j+1)2−i−1), c((j+1)2−i)}2<∞.

2 · Why the approximation is genuinely embedded

Choose p<q<2. Boedihardjo–Geng construct arbitrarily fine affine interpolants that remain Jordan and converge in q-variation. Each finite polygon is rounded inside pairwise disjoint vertex disks, on the same parameter circle, with an arbitrarily small change in 1-variation. The result is a regular C1 embedding, which Asano–Ike Remark 5.6 permits. No convolution of the original rough curve is assumed to remain embedded.

For c=(x,y), Young–Lœve continuity gives

Fn(t)=∫0tyndxn  → ∫0ty dx.

The period increments converge too, so the primitives converge locally uniformly on ℝ. This is precisely Asano–Ike Theorem 1.1.

At the exact critical scale, Antonelli–Young prove convergence of signed area over all fine partitions. Two prefix partitions of [0,t] can be extended by the same fine tail on [t,1]; that tail cancels. Their total-area Cauchy estimate is therefore uniform in t and supplies the complete local primitive. The same embedded polygons and diagonal C1 rounding then apply.

3 · A strict nonrectifiable witness

For 1<d<2, join two rotated spirals

γ0(θ)=θ−1/deiθ,   γ1(θ)=eiδγ0(θ),   θ≥1,

by an outer circular arc. Monotone radius proves embeddedness. Its length diverges like Σn−1/d, but its p-variation is finite for every p>d. Every projection oscillates infinitely often at the common origin, so it is not locally monotone.

A stronger critical witness uses disjoint many-turn annular detours with

an=2−n−20,   Nn=⌈4n/n2⌉,   wn=Nnan2.

The summable times wn give a ½-Hölder clock. A supported-bump estimate gives finite Antonelli–Young energy, while ΣNnanp=∞ for every p<2. Disjoint disks prove the curve is Jordan; accumulating full turns make it non-locally-monotone.

4 · The critical p=2 obstruction

For any Jordan trace C=∂Ω, inner conformal level curves converge with parameter and have Liouville periods tending to −|Ω|. Outer conformal level curves converge to the same parametrization, but their periods tend to −|Ω̄|=−|Ω|−|C|. When |C|>0, interleaving the two smooth Jordan sequences destroys primitive convergence.

Critical counterexample. A scale-controlled Hilbert–Osgood construction gives a positive-area ½-Hölder Jordan curve, hence one of finite 2-variation. Thus finite 2-variation plus parameter-aligned embedded C0 approximation does not automatically control primitives.

This refutes stability for a supplied approximation sequence, not the existential Asano–Ike criterion, and is not a counterexample to Square Peg. For zero-area traces the total periods are stable. The quadratic-diameter theorem now controls one strict critical local-lift class; curves outside that class remain the frontier.

5 · Unrestricted zero-trace obstruction

Asano–Ike already handle every positive-measure Jordan trace by density, so the unrestricted problem reduces to null traces. Put

a(θ)=θ−1/2,   b(θ)=(a(θ)+a(θ+2π))/2.

Following the a-spiral inward and the interleaved b-spiral outward gives a null Jordan curve with finite enclosed area: a(θ)>b(θ)>a(θ+2π), while a(θ)2−b(θ)2=O(θ−2). But its inward action is

½∫θ₀Θθ−1dθ =½ log(Θ/θ₀).
Obstruction theorem. This null Jordan curve admits no parameter-aligned regular C1 Jordan approximants with locally uniformly convergent Liouville primitives.

A tubular-crosscut rigidity lemma forces any limiting primitive to equal the classical integral on every smooth subarc, contradicting continuity at the spiral’s origin parameter. Thus the Asano–Ike primitive criterion is not universal. This is not a counterexample to Square Peg: the remaining target is their weaker diagonal μhom-cohomology vanishing at θ=π/2.

6 · Universal conformal L2 compactness

Universal weak-lift theorem. For every bounded Jordan domain, the analytic level curves of its Riemann map have mean-centered Liouville primitives converging strongly in L2 in the harmonic-measure parameter.

If f(z)=Σanzn and cr(t)=f(reit), the positive Fourier coefficients of the periodic α-primitive are

P̂r(k)=Cr,k/(4i)+Dr,k/(2ik),   |Dr,k|≤|Ω|/π.

Carathéodory convergence controls C in ℓ2; the 1/k factor controls D. Parseval gives strong L2 convergence. If these primitives are asymptotically equicontinuous, convergence is uniform and Asano–Ike supplies every rectangle. Otherwise order-one action concentrates on shrinking prime-end arcs, whose closed winding multiplicity grows at least as the inverse square of their image diameter.

Sharp concentration model. The null spiral carries fixed action ε on cells with area O(V−1), critical trace capacity O(V−2), and harmonic measure exp(−Θ(V2)).

Its divergent primitive is compressed in harmonic parameter to ¼ log log(1/|s−s0|)+O(1). Thus no estimate based only on finite Lp, conformal W1,2 energy, harmonic measure, or critical capacity can remove the exceptional action.

7 · Persistent diagonal locality

Individual shrinking-square actions are not controlled: four disjoint fixed-vertex Hamiltonian twists preserve an exact shrinking square while changing its action by an arbitrary four-point second difference. The required unrestricted theorem is therefore bar-level.

Exact open lemma. If L0∩Q(L0)=Δ0, prove that RΓ(ρ−1Δ0; μhom(F0,TaQF0))=0 for a∉πℤ.

Equivalently, all interior-action generators collapsing to the diagonal must pair into persistence bars whose lengths vanish and leave no exact-action endpoint microstalk. Small local Hamiltonian oscillation alone does not prove this, because microlocal Hom need not commute with the shrinking-neighborhood limit.

For every finite smooth null-spiral truncation, the diagonal complex lies only at action 0 mod π. In the limit its common phase winds through the full action circle while the sheet gap is π/(2θ)+O(θ−2). The original collapsed-point eye model was incorrect: the actual restriction retains the full action circle, the !-versus-* cone has τt=0, and the deep eye is arbitrarily torsion. The exact missing datum is a possible Milnor boundary term in the metric-completion telescope.

The interval k[a,a+ε) converges to zero while keeping a nonzero microstalk at its fixed left endpoint. An action skyscraper likewise has zero one-sided interleaving distance but nonzero cohomology. Hence an arbitrarily small Hofer cutoff of the diagonal germ does not prove the required vanishing.

Matschke’s every-scale trapezoids also strengthen to a connected fixed-type continuum spanning every compact positive scale interval. Local prime-end topology excludes none of its collision screens, so any useful configuration obstruction must be global along this continuum.

8 · Square-envelope area conservation

For outer paths a,b, put v=b−a and take the opposite square vertices c=a+Jv, d=b+Jv. The two closed ribbons obey

Ain−Aout =(‖v(s)‖2−‖v(t)‖2)/2.

The long-side integrands cancel identically; only the straight end connectors remain. Thus a compactified finite-p-variation square envelope with simple nested ribbons is impossible. Hugelmeyer’s envelope does not currently supply simplicity, the required outer/inner winding bounds, or a joint rough lift, so this is an exact conditional obstruction rather than a proof of the general conjecture.

9 · Three unrestricted no-go theorems

Microlocal telescope. Let F=k(0,1) and Gn=k(1/n,1). Then hocolim Gn=F and every finite microlocal restriction over 0 vanishes, but μhom(F,F) has a rank-one boundary microstalk. Thus the composite is not cocontinuous even for a fixed bounded finite-stalk constructible input; both the internal-Hom germ and microlocal specialization obstruct a generic theorem. Oh’s engulfable comparison concerns a cotangent fundamental class under a full engulfable homotopy, whereas Asano–Ike’s v maps to v⊗1+1⊗v and restricts to zero on the clean diagonal.

Envelope winding. For z(t)=exp(−t+(i/20)sin(πt/log2)), the square vertices (z,2z,(1+i)z,(2+i)z) satisfy full outer–inner cross-time avoidance and can be separated by a triangular Jordan domain. Nevertheless a(n log2)=b((n+1)log2), producing disjoint lenses with alternating winding signs.

Wild collision degree. An explicit square-test zero has Jacobian determinant −2. Scaled embedded copies collapse into a locally square-free ¼-Lipschitz Jordan graph while retaining an exact square screen and fixed prime-end side. For parameter-aligned uniform Jordan approximations, the total-collision orbit of the cyclic gap simplex can absorb odd local square degree.

These results exclude three formal shortcuts. They do not solve the conjecture; the live targets require a GKS-specific boundary theorem, a global two-ended envelope invariant, or a configuration degree with a nonlocal boundary correction.

10 · Sharper boundary calculations and a conormal module

Positive GKS is insufficient. A smooth exact-graph telescope has invertible compactly supported GKS stages, summable Hamiltonian amplitudes, and canonical positive epigraph maps. Every finite restriction at one reduced covector vanishes, but the homotopy colimit is the original object and has a rank-one restriction. Any surviving theorem must use the conjugated quarter-rotation and its four-sheet cap map, not positivity alone.

The global boundary invariants are exact but permissive. The two exterior envelope strands have end integers n−, n+ with n−−n+=1. This is an allowed end-order reversal of two disjoint proper cylinder arcs. The cyclic configuration quotient is nonorientable: its global degree group is Z/2, while the resolved collision boundary has an integer whose inclusion is reduction mod 2. The wild motif realizes one boundary unit with exact equivariant determinant −8.

Conormal forcing. A July 2026 unrefereed manuscript claiming Tao’s four-curve alternating-area conjecture has a mandatory sign error: it defines A=−∫y dx and then uses the opposite normalization. With Tao’s convention A=+∫y dx, two independent audits found no deeper gap in the shear, exact-circle, cutoff, conormal-PSS, or smoothing steps. This is an apparently sound auxiliary claim after repair, not established literature.

It does not solve Square Peg. Horizontal essential circles at heights (0,ε,3ε,2ε) have zero alternating action and exact joint squares (x,0), (x+2ε,ε), (x+ε,3ε), (x−ε,2ε), which totally collide under natural decoding as ε→0. The full rank-two conormal Floer group can likewise be realized in an arbitrarily small neighborhood of the zero conormal. For this family the normalized action is zero while the Tao-cylinder (universal-cover) side is √5ε, so the same action supports both collision and escape to infinity. The missing upgrade is a nonlocal relative class or a decoder-calibrated spectral gap.

Sharp surviving splice. Retain four compact arcs of the planar curve with empty common intersection and complete them by artificial winding tails. Put the retained pieces in one lifted disk with horizontal diameter plus vertical diameter smaller than the cylinder period, so retained cylinder squares cannot wrap. If the transverse balanced quadruple has exactly one ordered square with any artificial vertex, rank HF=2 forces a second square entirely on the retained arcs; compactness and the empty intersection exclude total collision. A two-zero graph model gives an exact baseline for this construction; generic offsets separate the two four-point base sets. The unproved step is an odd artificial splice lemma: four connector corridors and the action-correction ear must create no mixed square. This reduces to fifteen explicit incidence-pattern congruence systems, not genericity.
