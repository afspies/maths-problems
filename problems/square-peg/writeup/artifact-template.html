Rectangular pegs from Young-regular Jordan curves

A finite-variation bridge to the Asano–Ike criterion

partial result problems/square-peg 2026-07-24 unrestricted conjecture open
Abstract We combine Asano–Ike’s 2026 smooth-approximation criterion with Boedihardjo–Geng’s embedded polygon interpolation and Young integration. Every Jordan parametrization of finite p-variation, p < 2, inscribes every prescribed rectangle. A Dini–Young hypothesis reaches the critical scale. An explicit double spiral shows that the class includes curves that are neither rectifiable nor locally monotone. At p=2, a positive-area ½-Hölder Jordan curve gives incompatible inner and outer embedded primitive limits. More sharply, a planar-null double spiral admits no parameter-aligned C1 approximation with convergent primitives. Its conformal exhaustion concentrates fixed action at zero critical trace capacity, producing an explicit vanishing-width microlocal eye. The unrestricted conjecture remains open.

2 · Proof architecture

Choose p < q < 2. Boedihardjo–Geng construct arbitrarily fine affine interpolants that remain Jordan and converge in q-variation. Each finite polygon is rounded inside pairwise disjoint vertex disks, on the same parameter circle, with an arbitrarily small change in 1-variation. The result is a regular C1 embedding, which Asano–Ike Remark 5.6 permits. Embeddedness is therefore proved, not assumed.

Young–Lœve continuity gives uniform convergence of the normalized Liouville primitives on one period. Their period increments converge too, so the primitives converge locally uniformly on ℝ. These are exactly the hypotheses of Asano–Ike Theorem 1.1.

3 · Strict nonrectifiable witness

For 1 < d < 2, take two rotated spirals

γ0(θ)=θ−1/deiθ,   γ1(θ)=eiδγ0(θ),   θ≥1, (2)

and join their radius-one endpoints by a circular arc. Monotone radius proves embeddedness. Length diverges like Σn−1/d, while p-variation is finite for every p > d. Every linear projection oscillates infinitely often at the common origin, so the curve is not locally monotone.

4 · Critical p = 2 frontier

For any Jordan trace C=∂Ω, inner conformal level curves converge with parameter and have Liouville periods tending to −|Ω|. Outer conformal level curves converge to the same parametrization, but their periods tend to −|Ω̄|=−|Ω|−|C|. When |C|>0, interleaving the two smooth Jordan sequences destroys primitive convergence.

Critical counterexample. A scale-controlled Hilbert–Osgood construction gives a positive-area ½-Hölder Jordan curve, hence one of finite 2-variation. Thus finite 2-variation plus parameter-aligned embedded C0 approximation does not automatically control primitives.

This refutes stability for a supplied approximation sequence, not the existential Asano–Ike criterion, and is not a counterexample to Square Peg. For zero-area traces the total periods are stable; local subarc area lifts remain the frontier.

5 · Unrestricted zero-trace obstruction

Asano–Ike already handle every positive-measure Jordan trace by density, so the unrestricted problem reduces to null traces. Put

a(θ)=θ−1/2,   b(θ)=(a(θ)+a(θ+2π))/2.

Following the a-spiral inward and the interleaved b-spiral outward gives a null Jordan curve with finite enclosed area. Its inward action nevertheless grows as ½ log(Θ/θ₀).

Obstruction theorem. This null Jordan curve admits no parameter-aligned regular C1 Jordan approximants with locally uniformly convergent Liouville primitives.

A tubular-crosscut rigidity lemma forces any limiting primitive to equal the classical integral on every smooth subarc, contradicting continuity at the origin parameter. The Asano–Ike primitive criterion is therefore not universal. This is not a counterexample to Square Peg; the remaining target is their weaker diagonal μhom-cohomology vanishing at θ=π/2.

6 · Universal conformal L2 compactness

Universal weak-lift theorem. For every bounded Jordan domain, the analytic level curves of its Riemann map have mean-centered Liouville primitives converging strongly in L2 in the harmonic-measure parameter.

If f(z)=Σanzn and cr(t)=f(reit), the positive Fourier coefficients of the periodic α-primitive are

P̂r(k)=Cr,k/(4i)+Dr,k/(2ik),   |Dr,k|≤|Ω|/π.

Carathéodory convergence controls C in ℓ2; the 1/k factor controls D. Parseval gives strong L2 convergence. If these primitives are asymptotically equicontinuous, convergence is uniform and Asano–Ike supplies every rectangle. Otherwise order-one action concentrates on shrinking prime-end arcs, whose closed winding multiplicity grows at least as the inverse square of their image diameter.

Sharp concentration model. The null spiral carries fixed action ε on cells with area O(V−1), critical trace capacity O(V−2), and harmonic measure exp(−Θ(V2)).

Its divergent primitive is compressed in harmonic parameter to ¼ log log(1/|s−s0|)+O(1). Thus no estimate based only on finite Lp, conformal W1,2 energy, harmonic measure, or critical capacity can remove the exceptional action.

7 · Persistent diagonal locality

Individual shrinking-square actions are not controlled: four disjoint fixed-vertex Hamiltonian twists preserve an exact shrinking square while changing its action by an arbitrary four-point second difference. The required unrestricted theorem is therefore bar-level.

Exact open lemma. If L0∩Q(L0)=Δ0, prove that RΓ(ρ−1Δ0; μhom(F0,TaQF0))=0 for a∉πℤ.

Equivalently, all interior-action generators collapsing to the diagonal must pair into persistence bars whose lengths vanish and leave no exact-action endpoint microstalk. Small local Hamiltonian oscillation alone does not prove this, because microlocal Hom need not commute with the shrinking-neighborhood limit.

For every finite smooth null-spiral truncation, the diagonal complex lies only at action 0 mod π. In the limit its common phase winds through the full action circle while the sheet gap is π/(2θ)+O(θ−2). The original collapsed-point model was incorrect: the full action circle remains, the !-versus-* cone has τt=0, and the deep eye is arbitrarily torsion. The exact missing datum is an exact-action Milnor term in the completion telescope.

The interval k[a,a+ε) converges to zero while keeping a nonzero microstalk at its fixed left endpoint. An action skyscraper likewise has zero one-sided interleaving distance but nonzero cohomology. Hence an arbitrarily small Hofer cutoff of the diagonal germ does not prove the required vanishing.

Matschke’s every-scale trapezoids also strengthen to a connected fixed-type continuum spanning every compact positive scale interval. Local prime-end topology excludes none of its collision screens, so any useful configuration obstruction must be global along this continuum.

8 · Square-envelope area conservation

For outer paths a,b, put v=b−a and take the opposite vertices a+Jv, b+Jv. The two closed ribbons satisfy (Ain−Aout)= (‖v(s)‖2−‖v(t)‖2)/2. Thus a compactified square envelope with simple nested ribbons is impossible. Hugelmeyer’s theorem does not provide simplicity, the required outer/inner winding bounds, or a joint rough lift, so this is an exact conditional obstruction.

9 · Exact hygiene checks

The finite harness does not prove the analytic theorem. It checks rational polygon simplicity, Liouville/shoelace primitives under subdivision, and candidate squares exactly.
