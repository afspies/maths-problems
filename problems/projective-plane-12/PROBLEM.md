# Projective plane of order 12

## Statement
Does a projective plane of order 12 exist? That is: 157 points, 157 lines, 13 points
per line, 13 lines per point, any two points on exactly one common line. Equivalent:
a symmetric 2-(157, 13, 1) design, or a complete set of 11 mutually orthogonal Latin
squares of order 12.

## Status / context
Order 10 was ruled out by Lam–Thiel–Swiercz (1989) via years of CPU time on
error-correcting-code case analysis. Order 12 is open in both directions:
n = 12 is not a prime power (no classical construction) and is not excluded by
Bruck–Ryser (12 ≡ 0 mod 4, so B-R is silent). Best partial: N(12) ≥ 5 MOLS known;
a full set of 11 is required for a plane.

## Certificate + verifier
- **Existence**: a 157×157 0/1 incidence matrix N with N Nᵀ = 12·I + J (integer
  arithmetic, instant). Or 11 MOLS of order 12 — also instantly checkable.
- **Non-existence**: no finite certificate in general — but the Lam-style route
  (codes generated by the incidence matrix + exhaustive case analysis) can in
  principle be made a machine-checked proof; that's a mega-project, flag but don't
  promise.

## Approach notes
- The tractable model-guided angle is the MOLS ladder: push N(12) from 5 upward.
  Each new MOLS-set record is itself a publishable finite certificate.
- Structured searches: assume a nontrivial automorphism group (tactical
  decompositions) — planes of order 12 with various prescribed automorphisms have
  been excluded piecemeal in the literature; map what's excluded, search the gaps.
  Prescribed-automorphism searches reduce to modest-size exact cover / SAT instances.
- Deliverables: verifier suite (incidence, MOLS, code-theoretic invariants),
  reproduction of a known exclusion (e.g. order 12 with an automorphism of order 13),
  new exclusions or MOLS records.

## First steps
1. Verifier for symmetric 2-(157,13,1) incidence matrices + MOLS checker.
2. Literature map of excluded automorphism groups for order 12 (Janko et al.).
3. Pick the smallest unexcluded automorphism assumption; encode as exact cover/SAT.
