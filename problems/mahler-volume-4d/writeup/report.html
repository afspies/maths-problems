Four-dimensional Mahler volume conjecture

Sharp joins, exact terminal-bridge counterexample, and certified realization saddles

partial results problems/mahler-volume-4d 24 July 2026
Abstract We audit the new three-dimensional shadow-flow proof, then disprove the proposed terminal classification bridge with an exact rational 24-cell. Exact beta factorizations settle every four-dimensional affine join and strictly separate all products and free sums from equality. An exact Santaló-reduced Hessian excludes an open realization neighborhood of the regular 24-cell; interval covariance excludes a second nonregular branch. Closed covariance formulas exclude eight smooth 24-cell curves, and a q-regular stress arc enters an open smooth pair-terminal family. Exact KKT stress correction, projective-radical, spanning-germ, and boundary-trace reductions now isolate the connected case. The general 4D conjecture remains open.

1 · The terminal-pair bridge is false

Theorem 0. There is a rational non-simplex 4-polytope Q in Santaló position such that both Q and Q° admit only globally affine admissible speeds in every direction.

Take the Paffenholz 24-cell realization with a=(1/5,2/5,3/5,4/5), put g=c(P0°), and set

Q = {x/(1−g·x) : x∈P0}.

Directly from polarity, Q°=P0°−g, so the origin is exactly s(Q). Exhaustive facet-normal-flat enumeration gives 1,941 primal and 580 polar direction types; every speed space has dimension five. Two independent exact triangulations agree on both centroids.

3 · Sharp result for every 4D affine join

Theorem 1. Every four-dimensional affine join K*L satisfies ℘(K*L) ≥ 3125/576, with equality exactly for a 4-simplex.

For dimensions p, q and d=p+q+1, beta integration at the Santaló height τ=(q+1)/(d+1) gives

℘(K*L) = ((p!q!/d!)2 (d+1)d+1 / ((p+1)p+1(q+1)q+1)) ℘(K)℘(L).

The factor exactly transports the lower-dimensional simplex constants. The only non-pyramidal 4D split is 1+2; equality forces a segment and a triangle, whose join is a simplex. Similarly,

℘(K×L) = ℘(K⊕L) = (p!q!/(p+q)!) ℘(K)℘(L).

The 2+2 lower bound is 243/32 and the 1+3 lower bound is 64/9, both strictly above 3125/576.

This is a realization-moduli quotient. Only the 20 affine directions are Mahler gauges; the remaining four projective directions carry the covariance Hessian.

7 · Surviving terminal subclasses

For a facet F, admissibility is the family of exact equations λTα|F=0 over all affine dependences λ of its vertices. Choosing a direction parallel to a largest facet gives, for every pair-terminal 4-polytope,

2f03 ≥ 5(f0+f3)−10.

The supporting flag identity is f03−f02+2f2=2f3.

Theorem 4. A terminal simplicial 4-polytope is a simplex. A pair-terminal simple 4-polytope is a simplex. A pair-terminal 4-pyramid is a simplex.

For pyramids the key is exact descent: A(u,0)(pyr Q) ≅ Au(Q)⊕ℝ. The polar is again a pyramid over a dual 3-polytope, so the audited 3D dual-pair count forces a tetrahedral base.

8 · Source audit

The Meyer–Reisner convexity/rigidity theorem, volume-affinity, compactness, and the minimizer/polar chain are dimension-independent. The printed Chen–Li–Xi–Xu persistence proof follows intersections of two and three facets and is specifically 3D. In 4D, choose four independent active moved facets: their limiting intersection and the moved old vertex give a dimension-free replacement. The analytic reduction survives, but terminal classification cannot be the global bridge; full second variation is the remaining obstacle.

10 · Limits and next route

Generic rigidity is too weak: the cube is rigid generically but not in facet-parallel directions. More decisively, exact pair-terminality does not force a simplex, and a raw singular tangent need not integrate. Ordinary circuit Poincaré, cellwise slack bounds, and facet-pair positivity are now exactly ruled out. The next campaign must prove or falsify the global boundary transport inequality on the connected circuit-support branch, and apply the stress-corrected Schur complement to any candidate passing the projective covariance test.
